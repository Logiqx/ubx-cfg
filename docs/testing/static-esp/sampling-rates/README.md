## Static ESP Testing - Sampling Rates

- Google "gaussian error propagation reduction in error with twice as many measurements"
  - Doubling the number of measurements (N) reduces the error of the mean by a factor of sqrt(2).
  - Because error scales inversely with sqrt(N), taking twice as many measurements decreases your uncertainty to about 70.7 of its original value.
- Assumptions... neither are true for GPS data
  - The errors are random and normally distributed
  - The measurements are independent of one another
